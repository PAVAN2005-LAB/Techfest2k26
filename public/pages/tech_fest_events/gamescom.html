TEAMS AND ROSTERS
Each team’s roster must consist of at least 5 players.
Match must be played in the following format: 5v5.
A player is allowed to represent only one Team.

PROGRESSION
Gameplay will be in the form of a Single Elimination Knockout Tournament. A single-elimination, knockout, or sudden-death tournament is a type of elimination tournament where the loser of each match-up is immediately eliminated from the tournament.
Each winner will play another in the next round, until the final match-up, whose winner becomes the tournament champion.
All Teams must be present in the Campus at the time of the event.

Mortal Combat

CHARACTER SELECTION
The winning player, of the preceding match, will choose their character first, for the upcoming round
The losing player, of the preceding match, will choose their character last, for the upcoming round.
