Death Rush

The death rush is actually the competition between the robots. There will be an obstacle race in which various types of obstacles will be given to player to which they have to clear in minimum amount of time. Further will be a match between robots which is basically a football match and it will end with a death battle arena for robots


ARENA OF ROUND ONE

In round one the bot has to go on a straight path and then the hurdles will start, firstly it will encounter gravel, then there will be zigzag turning paths, forward to this there is ramp which is like a see-saw, then a small Cosmo work i.e., displacing box into the wholes Time required for round one should be between 12- 15 min.

2 More rounds will be conducted after the Round one.

Rules

  1. Anyone who wants to play, each team should have 4 members.
  2. The dimensions of the bot should be less than or equal to (length = 20 to 40 cm, breadth = 15 to 30cm, height = 5 to 15 cm) failing which the team will be disqualified from the competition (this excludes the dimension of the gripper but includes tires).
  3. Bot can be wired or wirelessly controllable. In the case of wired bots, the length of the wire should be in between (1.5 to 3.5 m) so that the wire remains slack at any instant of time.
  4. Fully furnished market robot is not allowed.
  5. Weight of the bot should be lesser than 5kg.
  6. The Bot must have an onboard power supply in any case.

Terms and Conditions

If there is any kind of damage to any equipment, then the participant will be responsible for it and will compensate for it.
