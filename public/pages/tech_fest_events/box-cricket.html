Box Cricket

In this event the teams play cricket in a nutshell. The participated teams will play cricket in provided small area in net.


Game Format

The match is played between the two teams. There are 6 players in each team(1 or 2 extra players).There are 6 overs per inning, the rules for box cricket is same as others box cricket league. The time limit for the game is approximately 35 minutes. In this time which team wins maximum matches will be declare as a winner.
